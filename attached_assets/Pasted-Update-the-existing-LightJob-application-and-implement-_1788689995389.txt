8. TASK FUND RESERVATION AND REFUND RULE

When an advertiser creates a task, the required task budget is RESERVED.

The reserved money becomes:

"Locked Task Funds"

The advertiser cannot withdraw or reuse the locked task funds while the task is active.

IMPORTANT REFUND RULE:

The task budget is NON-REFUNDABLE for the first 4 DAYS after the task is created.

During those first 4 days:

- Advertiser cannot cancel the task for a refund.
- Advertiser cannot withdraw the reserved task funds.
- Reserved funds remain locked.

After 4 days:

If some or all task slots were NOT completed, the system may make the unused portion eligible for refund according to the platform's refund rules.

Example:

Advertiser creates a task for:

100 workers × ₦400 = ₦40,000.

After 4 days:

60 workers completed the task.

40 workers remain.

The used portion:
60 × ₦400 = ₦24,000

Unused portion:
40 × ₦400 = ₦16,000

The unused ₦16,000 can become:

"Refund Eligible"

and may be returned to the advertiser's Available Balance after the applicable task/refund process.

IMPORTANT:
Money already distributed for valid completed tasks must NEVER be refunded to the advertiser.
